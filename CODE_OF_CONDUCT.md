# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good faith, critique work rather than people, and keep discussions on topic. Harassment, personal attacks and publishing others' private information are not tolerated in issues, pull requests, discussions or any field operated by the maintainers.

Report conduct concerns privately to justin@viridisconservation.com. Reports are handled confidentially, and maintainers may remove content or block participants who violate this code.
